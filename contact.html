The Dorn lab continuously seeks talented researchers from all backgrounds who are excited to join our research efforts! See below for guidance on how to get involved:

Open

Graduate Students

Prospective graduate students are encouraged to reach out to Prof. Dorn by email to schedule a video call, and must apply through the UVM Chemistry department graduate program in order to be considered for admission.

Open

Postdoctoral Researchers

Applications should be sent directly to Prof. Dorn via email. Please include a curriculum vitae, 2-3 representative publications, and the contact information of 3 professional references in order to receive full consideration.

Open

Undergraduate Students

UVM undergraduate students who have taken organic chemistry and are interested in organic/organometallic methodology research should contact Prof. Dorn by email.
